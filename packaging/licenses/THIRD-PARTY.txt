FramePlayerX bundles these libraries as separate shared objects in lib/:

FFmpeg 7.1.1 (libavformat, libavcodec, libavutil, libswresample, libswscale)
  Licence: GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1 or later (LGPL-2.1-or-later),
  full text in LGPL-2.1.txt. Built without any GPL or non-free components.
  Source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.1.tar.xz
  (also http://archive.ubuntu.com/ubuntu/pool/universe/f/ffmpeg/ffmpeg_7.1.1.orig.tar.xz)
  Exact build recipe and configure flags: tools/build-ffmpeg.sh in the
  FramePlayerX repository, https://github.com/remotesojourner/frameplayerx
  Modified: libavformat/hls.c lets http(s) playlist and segment URLs through
  to the application's own I/O callback (tools/ffmpeg-patches/ in the
  repository); everything else is the release as published.
  You may replace the libraries in lib/ with your own build of the same
  major versions (libavcodec.so.61, libavformat.so.61, libavutil.so.59,
  libswresample.so.5, libswscale.so.8).

Rust crates compiled into the frameplayerx binary are under MIT, Apache-2.0,
BSD, ISC, Zlib, Unicode-3.0 and similar licences. rust-crates.txt lists them,
then gives the licence, copyright and notice files each one ships;
rust-crates-extra.txt has the licences of those that ship none.
