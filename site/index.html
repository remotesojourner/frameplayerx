A cinema for flat videos

Watch flat videos in a full-size cinema instead of on a floating screen: the Classic, a stadium hall with red velvet seats, or the Luxe, an open room with neon lights and leather recliners. Pick your row and how much the screen curves around you, and the house lights dim as the film starts, as far and as slowly as you like, and come up when you pause. How the cinema works.

Your videos, wherever they are

Browse any folder on the headset, a microSD card or a USB drive, including cards formatted on Windows or a Mac, and make libraries of your folders. Or add a server:

  • Jellyfin
  • Emby
  • Plex
  • DeoVR feed
  • HereSphere API
  • SMB shares
  • NFS shares
  • SFTP
  • WebDAV
  • DLNA
  • Web folders

Jellyfin, Emby and Plex can transcode while you watch and sync what you've watched. A DeoVR feed or HereSphere API transcodes when the server offers it, and HereSphere syncs watch state too.

Install FramePlayerX

It's free. Install it on the headset in Desktop Mode, with no PC needed, or from a PC.
