A look around

Made for watching in VR

Formats found for you

Flat, 180°, 360° and fisheye, in 2D or 3D, 3D Blu-rays included, picked from the file; one press changes it.

Comfort focus

What you look at stays whole as you tilt your head or lie down, in stereo 180° and fisheye videos.

Watching lying down

Recenter as you lie back, or let the screen and the menus follow your head.

Subtitles

From the video, a file beside it or OpenSubtitles, at the depth and size you set, and in 3D.

Passthrough videos

Your room around the people in passthrough videos, and behind green or blue screens.

Watch offline

Download from shares and media servers to the headset or a card, and watch with no connection.

Every feature

Get started

1. Install

On the headset in Desktop Mode, with no PC needed, or from a PC. How to install

2. Add your videos

A folder on the headset or a card, a network share or your media server, under Sources › Add source. Sources in the guide

3. Press play

The format is picked for you, and the control bar and the Adjust panel change anything else. The user guide

Help

Questions

Ask on Discord, or read the FAQ.

Translations
